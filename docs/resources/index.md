# UKAIFA computational and storage resource


The UKAIFA has access to a variety of computational resources in the EIDF hosted by EPCC at the Unviseristy of Edinburgh, providing a soverign AI capability to UK companies and the public sector instritutions.

Through the UKAIFA parter AI factory consortia HAMMERAI UKAIFA also has access to resources hosted by HLRS in Germany.


In addition to this through the wider EuroHPC JU initiative there is an ambition to establish routes to access to other AI factories compute capabilities during the course of this program.
